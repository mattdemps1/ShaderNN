About

A 6,256-param MLP defines a local update rule. Each cell reads its 3×3 neighbourhood, runs the network, and updates itself.


Weights are quantized to uint8 and stored as PNG images. The browser dequantizes and loads them into a WebGL2 uniform buffer.


Training runs in Python (build_ca.py) offline — no training in browser.
